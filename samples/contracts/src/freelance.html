FREELANCE SERVICE AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement is made and entered into this 3rd day of March 2026 in Makati City, Philippines, by and between BRIGHTLINE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS, INC., a corporation duly organized under Philippine law with office at 18F Ayala Tower, Makati City, represented by its President, Marco A. Villanueva (the “CLIENT”), and MARIA ISABEL S. REYES, of legal age, single, Filipino, with address at 42 Maginhawa St., Quezon City (the “FREELANCER”).

-W I T N E S S E T H-

That the CLIENT requires web design and development services, and the FREELANCER represents that she has the skill and experience to render them;

NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

  1. Services. The FREELANCER shall design and develop the CLIENT’s e-commerce website, including up to three (3) rounds of revisions, as described in Annex “A”.
  2. Fee and Payment. The CLIENT shall pay a total fee of ₱180,000.00, payable thirty percent (30%) upon signing and seventy percent (70%) upon completion. The CLIENT may withhold the final payment until it is fully satisfied with the work, in its sole judgment.
  3. Delivery. The work shall be delivered within sixty (60) days. For every day of delay, the FREELANCER shall pay liquidated damages of five percent (5%) of the total fee.
  4. Revisions. Revisions beyond the third round shall be performed by the FREELANCER free of charge until the CLIENT approves the work.
  5. Ownership of Work. All work, including drafts, concepts, and tools or code created or used by the FREELANCER before or during this engagement, shall belong exclusively to the CLIENT, in perpetuity and throughout the world, even if the CLIENT does not pay in full.
  6. Exclusivity and Non-Compete. For three (3) years after termination, the FREELANCER shall not render services to any person or company engaged in e-commerce or related business in the Philippines.
  7. Independent Contractor. The FREELANCER is an independent contractor and not an employee. She shall bear her own taxes, and shall be available to the CLIENT during regular office hours, using the equipment and following the instructions of the CLIENT.
  8. Termination. The CLIENT may terminate this Agreement at any time without notice or liability. The FREELANCER may terminate only upon sixty (60) days’ written notice and forfeits all unpaid fees.
  9. Indemnity. The FREELANCER shall indemnify the CLIENT against any and all claims, losses, and damages of any kind arising from this engagement, without limit.
  10. Confidentiality. The FREELANCER shall keep all information of the CLIENT confidential, during and for an indefinite period after this Agreement.
  11. Governing Law and Venue. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines. Any dispute shall be brought exclusively in the courts of Makati City.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date and place first written above.
