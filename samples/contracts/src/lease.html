CONTRACT OF LEASE

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Contract of Lease is made this 1st day of April 2026 at Cebu City, Philippines, by and between ROSARIO D. MENDOZA, of legal age, widow, Filipino, residing at 7 Jakosalem St., Cebu City, hereinafter called the LESSOR; and JOHN PAUL C. TAN, of legal age, single, Filipino, hereinafter called the LESSEE.

-W I T N E S S E T H-

That the LESSOR is the registered owner of a residential unit located at Unit 5B, Palm Residences, Mabolo, Cebu City, covered by Transfer Certificate of Title No. 045-2019001234;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing, the parties agree as follows:

  1. Term. The lease shall be for one (1) year, from April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027, renewable upon the written agreement of both parties.
  2. Rent. The LESSEE shall pay a monthly rent of ₱22,000.00 on or before the 5th day of each month. The LESSOR may increase the rent at any time upon seven (7) days’ notice.
  3. Deposit and Advance. The LESSEE shall pay three (3) months’ advance rent and three (3) months’ security deposit. The deposit shall be forfeited in full for any breach of this Contract and shall not be applied to unpaid rent.
  4. Late Payment. Rent paid after the due date shall bear a penalty of ten percent (10%) per day of delay.
  5. Use. The unit shall be used solely as a residence. The LESSEE shall not sublease, nor allow any person other than those named herein to stay in the unit.
  6. Repairs. The LESSEE shall shoulder all repairs, including structural repairs and those due to ordinary wear and tear.
  7. Entry. The LESSOR or her agents may enter the unit at any time, with or without notice.
  8. Termination. The LESSOR may terminate this Contract at any time for any reason upon three (3) days’ notice. Should the LESSEE terminate early, he shall pay the rent for the whole remaining term.
  9. Surrender. Upon expiration or termination, the LESSEE shall vacate immediately and waives the right to demand notice or to question any ejectment, and the LESSOR may dispose of any property left in the unit.
  10. Attorney’s Fees. In case of suit, the LESSEE shall pay attorney’s fees equivalent to twenty-five percent (25%) of the amount claimed, plus costs of suit.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands on the date and place first above written.

ROSARIO D. MENDOZA
Lessor
JOHN PAUL C. TAN
Lessee

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Before me, a Notary Public for and in Cebu City, personally appeared the above-named persons, who acknowledged that the same is their free and voluntary act and deed.
