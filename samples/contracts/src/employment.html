CONTRACT OF EMPLOYMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Contract is made on the 15th day of January 2026 in Pasig City, Philippines, by and between HARBOR POINT TRADING CORPORATION, with principal office at 25 Ortigas Avenue, Pasig City (the “EMPLOYER”), and ANGELO M. FERNANDEZ, of legal age, single, Filipino, residing at 9 Mabini St., Marikina City (the “EMPLOYEE”).

-W I T N E S S E T H-

That the EMPLOYER desires to hire the EMPLOYEE as Sales Associate, and the EMPLOYEE agrees to the following terms:

  1. Position and Status. The EMPLOYEE is engaged as Sales Associate on a probationary basis for six (6) months, which the EMPLOYER may extend for any further period it sees fit.
  2. Standards for Regularization. The EMPLOYEE shall become regular only if the EMPLOYER, in its sole discretion, finds his performance satisfactory. The standards for regularization need not be disclosed to the EMPLOYEE.
  3. Compensation. The EMPLOYEE shall receive a basic salary of ₱15,000.00 per month, inclusive of holiday pay, overtime pay, and all other benefits provided by law.
  4. Hours of Work. The EMPLOYEE shall work twelve (12) hours a day, six (6) days a week, and shall make himself available for additional work at any time without additional pay.
  5. Leave. The EMPLOYEE is not entitled to service incentive leave during the probationary period. Unused leave is forfeited at year-end without conversion to cash.
  6. Deductions. The EMPLOYER may deduct from the EMPLOYEE’s salary any shortage, loss, or damage to company property, as determined by the EMPLOYER, and the EMPLOYEE authorizes such deductions.
  7. Non-Compete. For five (5) years after separation for any reason, the EMPLOYEE shall not work for any business in the same industry anywhere in the Philippines.
  8. Training Bond. If the EMPLOYEE resigns within three (3) years, he shall pay the EMPLOYER ₱200,000.00 as liquidated damages.
  9. Termination. The EMPLOYER may terminate this Contract at any time without cause or notice. The EMPLOYEE shall render a ninety (90) day notice before resigning, failing which his final pay shall be forfeited.
  10. Quitclaim. Upon separation, the EMPLOYEE shall sign a quitclaim releasing the EMPLOYER from all claims, including those for unpaid wages.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Contract on the date and place first written above.
