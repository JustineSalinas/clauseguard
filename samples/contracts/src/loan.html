Republic of the Philippines

Department of Finance

BUREAU OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT FINANCE

CONTRACT OF LOAN

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This is a CONTRACT OF LOAN executed by and between the Province of Sulu Verde, represented by Governor Ramon T. Dela Vega, hereinafter known as the PARTY OF THE FIRST PART, and the Republic of the Philippines, represented by the Executive Director of the Bureau of Local Government Finance, Department of Finance, Administrator of the Assessment Loan Revolving Fund, established by virtue of Presidential Decree No. 464, as amended by Presidential Decree No. 1002, hereinafter known as the PARTY OF THE SECOND PART.

-W I T N E S S E T H-

That the PARTY OF THE FIRST PART having accomplished the documents and information required in the Assessment Regulations No. 4-77, dated May 23, 1977 of the Department of Finance;

That the PARTY OF THE FIRST PART having submitted to the PARTY OF THE SECOND PART all the required supporting papers incident to the application of loan;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the PARTY OF THE SECOND PART hereby grants and approves a loan in the amount of Five Million Pesos (₱5,000,000.00) Philippine Currency, out of the Assessment Loan Revolving Fund to the Province of Sulu Verde and that the PARTY OF THE FIRST PART hereby accepts said loan subject to the following terms and conditions:

  1. That the proceeds of the loan shall be expended on the basis of the estimated costs of the project as itemized in the Project Study and Action Plan (PSAP) submitted by the borrower Province, which Plan is hereby made an integral part hereof as Annex “A”;
  2. That the initial release of the proceeds of the loan shall be approximately 50% thereof; the date of the release and the amount thereof shall be scheduled and determined by the BLGF, Department of Finance;
  3. That the loan shall bear interest at the rate of six percent (6%) per annum, computed on the outstanding balance, and shall be payable in twenty (20) equal quarterly amortizations commencing one (1) year after the initial release;
  4. That in case of failure to pay any amortization on its due date, the PARTY OF THE FIRST PART shall pay a penalty charge of one percent (1%) per month on the amount due, without need of demand, and the PARTY OF THE SECOND PART may, at its sole option, declare the entire unpaid balance immediately due and demandable;
  5. That the PARTY OF THE SECOND PART may, at any time and without prior notice, suspend further releases or modify the terms of this Contract should it determine that the project is not being implemented in accordance with the PSAP;
  6. That the PARTY OF THE FIRST PART hereby authorizes the Department of Finance to deduct any unpaid amortization from its Internal Revenue Allotment, and waives any right to question such deduction;
  7. That any dispute arising from this Contract shall be filed exclusively before the proper court of Quezon City, the parties waiving any other venue.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands this ____ day of ____________ 2026 at Manila, Philippines.

RAMON T. DELA VEGA
Governor, Province of Sulu Verde
Party of the First Part
ATTY. LEONOR B. CASTILLO
Executive Director, BLGF
Party of the Second Part

ACKNOWLEDGMENT
